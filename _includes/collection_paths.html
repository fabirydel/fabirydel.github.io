{%- comment -%} Renders a collection's photo paths (relative to images/full, e.g. "bled/01_IMG_2855.jpg") joined by "|". Callers capture the output and split it: {%- capture joined -%}{% include collection_paths.html collection=c full_images=full_images %}{%- endcapture -%} {%- assign paths = joined | strip | split: "|" | uniq -%} A collection lists its photos explicitly under `images:`. The older trip-style collections list `locations:` instead; those expand to every photo of each location. Inputs: collection — a document from _collections/ full_images — static files under /images/full/ (only needed for `locations:`) {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cp_c = include.collection -%} {%- for cp_p in cp_c.images -%}{{ cp_p }}|{%- endfor -%} {%- if cp_c.locations -%} {%- for cp_loc in cp_c.locations -%} {%- assign cp_prefix = "/images/full/" | append: cp_loc | append: "/" -%} {%- assign cp_matches = include.full_images | where_exp: "img", "img.path contains cp_prefix" -%} {%- for cp_img in cp_matches -%}{{ cp_img.path | remove_first: "/images/full/" }}|{%- endfor -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endif -%}
